# Seedance 2.5 Couple Video Start｜双人情侣视频起手

## Purpose

Convert an approved couple image state into a short Seedance 2.5 motion start while preserving both identities and relationship chemistry.

The first frame must not merely be attractive; it must leave physically plausible motion space.

---

## VALIDATED FIRST-FRAME STATES

### LEAN-IN MOMENT — DEFAULT VIDEO START

Use as the current default first frame.

Properties:

- bodies already close but not at the minimum distance;
- one partner is clearly moving toward the other;
- waist / chest contact is readable but hands are not tangled;
- both faces remain separated enough for stable motion;
- the next motion direction is obvious.

Why it works:

- not a final state;
- provides room for slow approach;
- lower facial-collision risk than starting at almost-kiss distance;
- naturally progresses into pre-kiss pause.

---

### PRE-KISS PAUSE — SECOND KEYFRAME / TENSION HOLD

Use as a second keyframe or later beat rather than the default only first frame.

Properties:

- very small remaining face distance;
- strong eye-line and romantic tension;
- no completed kiss.

Risk:

- too little motion space;
- higher face-merging / mouth-nose collision risk;
- Seedance may complete the kiss too quickly.

---

### HELD EYE CONTACT — SAFE / SLOW START

Use when identity stability is more important than immediate motion.

Strength:

- clear faces;
- low collision risk;
- stable relationship read.

Weakness:

- can become visually static;
- requires a deliberate later lean-in cue.

---

## VALIDATED MOTION PATH

Current preferred path:

`LEAN-IN MOMENT → SLOW APPROACH → MICRO-PAUSE → PRE-KISS PAUSE`

Do not equate stronger romance with faster contact.

Current real generation comparison showed that the more effective result preserved unresolved distance and used lighting / pause to sustain anticipation. A more literal "more intimate" prompt progressed toward contact more directly and felt less ambiguous.

Core principle:

`ROMANTIC TENSION = APPROACH + RESTRAINT + UNRESOLVED DISTANCE`

not:

`ROMANTIC TENSION = COMPLETE CONTACT AS FAST AS POSSIBLE`

---

## Blocking Rules

For the validated face-to-face waist-hold start:

- partner is slightly more active in the approach;
- one hand stays stable at the user's waist / rear waist;
- user may keep one hand on partner chest / upper arm;
- both bodies move only slightly closer;
- head motion is small and continuous;
- gaze stays on each other;
- no sudden head turn;
- no hand sliding unless specifically designed;
- do not complete the kiss unless that beat is explicitly requested.

---

## Camera Rules

For the current 3–5 second start:

- medium close shot;
- stable or very subtle follow;
- no unnecessary cut;
- no sudden push / pull;
- keep both face profiles readable;
- camera motion must not compete with the intimacy action.

In current testing, a directional side/back window-light result created stronger facial separation and romantic atmosphere than a flatter high-key white backlight result.

Do not convert this into a universal lighting rule; it is a validated preference in the current setup.

---

## Identity Rules

Always isolate:

`PERSON_A = USER_REFERENCE_PACKAGE`

`PERSON_B = PARTNER_REFERENCE_PACKAGE`

For motion:

- preserve face geometry during approach;
- do not let near-face proximity blend identities;
- keep hairline / age / nose / jaw stable;
- hands must remain anatomically attached and contact must remain consistent.

---

## Prompt Structure for Seedance 2.5

Use concrete professional film instructions:

`START FRAME STATE → WHO MOVES FIRST → BODY ACTION → HAND STABILITY → HEAD / GAZE → DISTANCE CHANGE → END BEAT → CAMERA → LIGHT / REALISM → IDENTITY FAILSAFES`

Avoid abstract-only instructions such as:

- `make it more romantic`
- `more chemistry`
- `more sensual`

Translate them into physical action and timing.

---

## Current Default

For a short couple-video opening:

`LEAN-IN MOMENT first frame`

then:

`slow approach → unresolved pre-kiss pause`

For stronger tension, increase gaze hold / pause / micro-distance control before increasing contact.
