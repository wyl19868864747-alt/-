# Couple Moment DNA｜情侣甜蜜瞬间 DNA

## Purpose

The product goal is not a formal couple portrait. The image should feel like a **captured sweet relationship moment** with believable intimacy, chemistry and fantasy value.

Product feeling:

> “This is what my unknown best partner looks like.”

---

## Core Visual DNA｜VALIDATED FROM REFERENCE SET + GENERATION TESTS

### 1. Captured Moment, Not Formal Pose

Default:

- candid relationship moment
- imperfect but believable body arrangement
- attention partly on each other rather than only the camera
- natural asymmetry in posture

Avoid by default:

- passport-like symmetry
- stiff front-facing couple pose
- wedding-studio stiffness
- catalogue couple photography

### 2. Relationship Action Creates Sweetness

Sweetness must come from observable relationship behavior:

- leaning
- head proximity
- gaze
- protective body orientation
- shoulder / arm / waist proximity
- shared personal space

Do not rely only on attractive faces.

### 3. Shared Private Space

Intimacy increases when the two people visibly share the same personal space:

- minimal empty gap
- bodies naturally angle toward each other
- one person gives weight / trust toward the other
- two people read as one relationship unit

### 4. Romantic Tension Before Explicit Intimacy

Default emotional escalation:

```text
SWEETNESS
↓
INTIMACY
↓
ROMANTIC TENSION
↓
SOFT SENSUALITY
```

A small unresolved distance often creates more fantasy than immediately showing a direct kiss.

### 5. Couple Unit Composition

Compose `USER + PARTNER` as one visual unit, not two isolated portraits placed next to each other.

Useful cues:

- overlapping personal space
- head / shoulder relationship
- shared eyeline
- asymmetric but connected posture
- framing that binds both people together

---

## Product Priority

For final couple images, prioritize:

```text
RELATIONSHIP FEELING
>
SWEETNESS / CHEMISTRY
>
IDENTITY STABILITY
>
REALISM
>
INDIVIDUAL ATTRACTIVENESS
>
STYLING / LIGHTING
```

Identity stability remains a hard gate: chemistry never justifies changing the user’s identity.

---

## Chemistry Evaluation

Evaluate every final couple image on:

1. `SWEETNESS`
2. `COUPLE_LIKENESS`
3. `CHEMISTRY / ROMANTIC TENSION`
4. `PARTNER ATTRACTIVENESS`
5. `PHOTOREALISM`
6. `FANTASY / SHAREABILITY VALUE`

The result should not merely prove that two people are a couple. It should make the user want to keep looking at the moment.
