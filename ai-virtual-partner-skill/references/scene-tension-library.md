# Scene & Tension Library｜情侣场景与张力

## Purpose

Choose spaces that support believable intimacy. A private scene alone does not create chemistry; body relationship, gaze and unresolved distance matter more.

---

## VALIDATED SCENES

### WINDOW-SIDE — CURRENT STRONGEST

Observed result:

- strongest current romantic tension;
- natural reason for close standing positions;
- directional window light gives faces readable contour and separation;
- works especially well with `FACE-TO-FACE WAIST HOLD` and `BREATH-CLOSE`.

Best use:

- Hero image;
- first frame for Seedance lean-in motion;
- almost-kiss / eye-contact progression.

Risk:

- over-bright windows can flatten faces or create a white veil / haze;
- faces too close at the starting frame increase identity / collision risk.

---

### SOFA CORNER — VALIDATED REALISTIC

Observed result:

- strongest natural everyday-couple feeling;
- looks less posed;
- lower tension than window-side almost-kiss.

Best use:

- candid sweetness;
- stable relationship moments;
- conversational lean-in.

Risk:

- can become safe / domestic / low-tension if bodies remain side-by-side with no gaze or weight transfer.

---

### BEDROOM EDGE — VALIDATED PRIVATE, NOT AUTOMATICALLY MORE TENSE

Observed result:

- private-space signal is readable;
- waist contact works;
- current test became cheerful / sweet rather than more sensual.

Key rule:

`PRIVATE SPACE ≠ AUTOMATIC CHEMISTRY`

Expression, gaze, body distance and hand placement still determine tension.

Do not route to bedroom merely to make the image more intimate.

---

## Scene Routing

Current defaults:

- romantic Hero / heart-flutter → `WINDOW-SIDE`
- realistic daily sweetness → `SOFA CORNER`
- private relationship variant → `BEDROOM EDGE` only when the moment logic needs it

---

## Scene Color Expansion Status

The current validated tests overused neutral clothing / pale interiors. Color-rich scene families are **CANDIDATE / NOT YET VALIDATED** and should be tested separately.

Future color tests should change physical palette sources rather than adding vague `colorful` language:

- wardrobe hue;
- wall / textile hue;
- daylight time;
- practical lamp color;
- foliage / city-light background color;
- warm/cool separation.

Do not promote a color palette until its effect on skin realism, sweetness and identity stability has been tested.
