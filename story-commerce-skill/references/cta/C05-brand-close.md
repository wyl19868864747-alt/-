# C05 — Brand Close

USE WHEN: the ad's goal is brand memory, desire, launch awareness, or premium positioning rather than immediate hard conversion.
SEMANTIC ROLE: close on one memorable brand/product idea without forcing an inappropriate sales command.
COMMON FORMS: product / brand line, concise invitation, or clean end card tied to the verified brand message.
STYLE ADAPTATION: especially useful for quiet-luxury, fashion, cinematic, or image-led ads.
AVOID: empty slogan, invented brand promise, using a soft close when the user explicitly needs a direct conversion CTA.
