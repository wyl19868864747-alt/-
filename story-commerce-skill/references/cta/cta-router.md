# CTA Router

Purpose: convert the buying decision earned by the ad into one truthful next action.

Read `cta-index.md`, then load exactly one Primary CTA DNA.

## Selection Inputs

`Conversion Goal + real destination/action + audience friction + resolved Style + platform context`

CTA must use verified commercial facts. Do not invent urgency, scarcity, discount, eligibility, trial terms, shipping, or destination behavior.

## Current CTA Families

- C01 Direct Action
- C02 Low-Friction Explore
- C03 Qualification / Eligibility
- C04 Offer / Value Action
- C05 Brand Close

## Style Adaptation

Style may change:
- wording density
- performance tone
- graphic treatment
- spoken vs on-screen emphasis
- how hard or soft the final beat lands

Style may not change the real action being offered.

## CTA Judge

1. Is the next action unambiguous?
2. Is the action actually available?
3. Does it follow naturally from the decision/proof the ad just earned?
4. Is the friction level appropriate for the audience and offer?
5. Is the wording truthful and free of invented urgency?
6. Does the CTA presentation fit the resolved Style without becoming decorative noise?

Use one Primary CTA. Add one support line only when it removes a real friction point.
