# C03 — Qualification / Eligibility

USE WHEN: the real service requires qualification, eligibility, assessment, or fit checking before conversion.
SEMANTIC ROLE: turn interest into a truthful qualification step.
COMMON FORMS: See if you qualify / Check eligibility / Find out if it fits your needs.
REQUIREMENT: qualification process must actually exist and any time claim must be verified.
STYLE ADAPTATION: tone follows Style but should remain clear and trustworthy.
AVOID: implying guaranteed approval, medical outcome, or invented speed.
