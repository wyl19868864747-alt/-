# H04 — Visual Anomaly

PURPOSE: create immediate curiosity through one abnormal visual or physical event.
BEST WHEN: the anomaly can be causally tied to the selling point, product semantics, or story mechanism.
OPENING MECHANIC: establish enough reality to read the anomaly, then reveal the impossible/unexpected event.
VISUAL / PERFORMANCE DNA: one dominant anomaly; multiple angles may cover the same event.
STYLE ADAPTATION: anomaly can be raw, luxurious, editorial, action-driven, surreal, retro, etc.
PAYOFF OBLIGATION: explain or reclaim the anomaly through product meaning.
FAILURE MODES: random VFX, unrelated spectacle, product deformation, several competing anomalies.
