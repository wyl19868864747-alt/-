# H02 — Price Shock

PURPOSE: use a verified price/value fact as the primary attention asset.
BEST WHEN: the real number is genuinely decision-changing.
OPENING MECHANIC: surface the price/value contrast fast enough to be readable, then leave the reason or implication unresolved.
VISUAL / PERFORMANCE DNA: number/value is legible; human reaction remains believable.
STYLE ADAPTATION: price can appear conversational, elegant, graphic, cinematic, or absurd depending on Style.
PAYOFF OBLIGATION: exact price/offer conditions must remain consistent throughout.
FAILURE MODES: invented discount, fake urgency, price without context, oversized reaction that makes the fact feel fake.
