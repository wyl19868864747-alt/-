# H07 — Reaction First

PURPOSE: use a believable human reaction as the entry into a product-relevant event.
BEST WHEN: the product creates a naturally visible surprise, relief, desire, doubt, or recognition.
OPENING MECHANIC: show reaction and the stimulus relationship quickly; do not leave reaction floating without cause.
VISUAL / PERFORMANCE DNA: restrained micro-behavior before exaggerated acting.
STYLE ADAPTATION: performance intensity follows Style.
PAYOFF OBLIGATION: reveal the true stimulus and product reason.
FAILURE MODES: generic shocked face, screaming without cause, reaction used as fake proof of objective performance.
