# H05 — Misbelief

PURPOSE: make the viewer or character form one clear but incorrect assumption.
BEST WHEN: the product has a real misconception, hidden value, unexpected use, or perception gap.
OPENING MECHANIC: present evidence that naturally supports the wrong assumption without lying.
VISUAL / PERFORMANCE DNA: audience understands the assumption before it is corrected.
STYLE ADAPTATION: misdirection tone can be comedic, premium, noir, editorial, or social-native.
PAYOFF OBLIGATION: correction must reveal a true product fact or value.
FAILURE MODES: deception with no fair clue, twist unrelated to product, correcting a misconception nobody actually has.
