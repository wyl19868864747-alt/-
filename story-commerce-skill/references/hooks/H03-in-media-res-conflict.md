# H03 — In-Media-Res Conflict

PURPOSE: start while the character is already dealing with a relevant problem.
BEST WHEN: the product solves or changes an active friction point.
OPENING MECHANIC: begin after the problem has already started; no setup paragraph.
VISUAL / PERFORMANCE DNA: readable objective, obstruction, action, consequence.
STYLE ADAPTATION: conflict intensity and camera behavior follow Style.
PAYOFF OBLIGATION: product entry must causally alter the conflict.
FAILURE MODES: random argument, conflict unrelated to purchase decision, loud acting with no product causality.
