# ST08 — Y2K Pop

VISUAL CORE: early-2000s digital pop culture; youthful, glossy, playful, assertive.
EXPOSURE / COLOR: hard flash, silver, clear plastic, bright pink/blue/purple accents.
LIGHT: direct flash and reflective pop highlights.
CAMERA / TEXTURE: fisheye/ultrawide, early-digital camera feel, quick zoom, tilted framing, freeze-like cuts.
HOOK EXPRESSION BIAS: immediate graphic surprise, bold reaction, fast reveal.
STORY / CTA EXPRESSION BIAS: playful, punchy, short copy; CTA can land as a pop graphic beat.
ENVIRONMENT TENDENCY: bedroom, party, shop, street, studio, digital-pop space.
AVOID: unreadable cyber UI and excessive decorative clutter.
COMPRESSED STYLE LINE: Y2K pop with hard flash, silver and transparent materials, bright accent color, fisheye energy, early-digital texture, and punchy cuts.
