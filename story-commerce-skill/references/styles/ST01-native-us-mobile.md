# ST01 — Native U.S. Mobile Social Realism

VISUAL CORE: authentic U.S. social-native content; real-life imperfection, not polished TVC.
EXPOSURE / COLOR: normal exposure, natural white balance, realistic skin and product color.
LIGHT: window light and practical sources.
CAMERA / TEXTURE: phone wide/normal lens, light handheld follow, small reactive reframes.
HOOK EXPRESSION BIAS: immediate real-life event, spontaneous reaction, readable behavior.
STORY / CTA EXPRESSION BIAS: conversational and direct; CTA should feel native rather than announcer-like.
ENVIRONMENT TENDENCY: home, kitchen, street, car, workplace, store.
AVOID: excessive grading, perfect studio blocking, fake influencer overacting.
COMPRESSED STYLE LINE: Native U.S. mobile-social realism with natural light, real-life texture, light handheld following, and unpolished but readable framing.
