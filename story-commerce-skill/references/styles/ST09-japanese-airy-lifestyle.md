# ST09 — Japanese Airy Lifestyle

VISUAL CORE: clean, transparent, calm, observant everyday life with strong air and detail.
EXPOSURE / COLOR: bright high-key natural tones, white, pale wood, soft gray, muted green.
LIGHT: soft natural daylight with gentle shadows.
CAMERA / TEXTURE: stable observation, light slow push, static composition, shallow-depth detail.
HOOK EXPRESSION BIAS: quiet but instantly legible life problem, tactile action, or elegant before/after state.
STORY / CTA EXPRESSION BIAS: restrained emotion and low-pressure CTA; clarity over hype.
ENVIRONMENT TENDENCY: clean home, kitchen, workbench, street life, nature-adjacent spaces.
AVOID: washed-out product color, over-slow pacing, stereotype-driven casting.
COMPRESSED STYLE LINE: Japanese airy lifestyle with bright natural soft light, pale neutral materials, quiet observation, and clean everyday detail.
