# ST03 — Western High-Fashion Editorial

VISUAL CORE: strong styling, silhouette, pose, graphic composition, campaign/editorial tension.
EXPOSURE / COLOR: bold but controlled palette; sculptural hard flash, side light, or rim light when useful.
LIGHT: shape-driven editorial light.
CAMERA / TEXTURE: aggressive crops, low/high angles, punchy push/pull, pose-to-product cutting.
HOOK EXPRESSION BIAS: immediate visual attitude, bold pose/action or striking composition.
STORY / CTA EXPRESSION BIAS: less explanatory, more iconographic; CTA lands as fashion campaign copy when appropriate.
ENVIRONMENT TENDENCY: studio, architecture, street, backstage, minimal interiors.
AVOID: generic runway clichés, random luxury props.
COMPRESSED STYLE LINE: Western high-fashion editorial with sculptural light, bold styling, graphic crops, strong poses, and high-tension product close-ups.
