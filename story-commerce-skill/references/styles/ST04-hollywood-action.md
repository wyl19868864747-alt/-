# ST04 — Hollywood Action Blockbuster

VISUAL CORE: strong direction, movement, spatial depth, high-energy causality.
EXPOSURE / COLOR: high-contrast but readable real-world sources; preserve faces and product evidence.
LIGHT: directional back/rim light when motivated.
CAMERA / TEXTURE: tracking, fast push, short whip, low-angle wide motion; stabilize key result beats.
HOOK EXPRESSION BIAS: start inside an active problem or urgent physical event.
STORY / CTA EXPRESSION BIAS: momentum and decisive payoff; CTA feels like the final action beat.
ENVIRONMENT TENDENCY: any real task space can be action-cinematized.
AVOID: explosions, danger, or chase scenes without commercial cause.
COMPRESSED STYLE LINE: Hollywood action energy with strong depth, directional light, dynamic tracking and fast pushes, while every movement remains causally readable.
