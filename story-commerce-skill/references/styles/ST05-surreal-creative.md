# ST05 — Surreal Creative Advertising

VISUAL CORE: one clear impossible mechanism inside a believable real photographed world.
EXPOSURE / COLOR: reality-first exposure; impossible elements inherit real light and material behavior.
LIGHT: world-consistent source lighting.
CAMERA / TEXTURE: establish reality first, then reveal scale, spatial, suspension, transformation, or metaphor anomaly.
HOOK EXPRESSION BIAS: visual anomaly with immediate causal curiosity.
STORY / CTA EXPRESSION BIAS: spectacle must resolve back to product meaning and conversion, not remain decorative.
ENVIRONMENT TENDENCY: ordinary real spaces transformed by one impossible event.
AVOID: random particles, generic light ribbons, full fantasy backgrounds, product deformation, unrelated destruction.
COMPRESSED STYLE LINE: Grounded surreal advertising: a believable real-world photographic base disrupted by one clear impossible visual mechanism that remains physically integrated and product-relevant.
