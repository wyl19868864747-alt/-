# ST10 — Mediterranean Sun Holiday

VISUAL CORE: bright, relaxed, open, sun-rich lifestyle advertising.
EXPOSURE / COLOR: luminous blue/white, warm skin, stone/plaster/water reflections.
LIGHT: strong natural sun with breathable highlights and backlight.
CAMERA / TEXTURE: wide compositions, natural follow, occasional light slow motion, wind and air movement.
HOOK EXPRESSION BIAS: attractive lifestyle action, sunlit reveal, motion or sensory atmosphere tied to product use.
STORY / CTA EXPRESSION BIAS: easy, aspirational, low-friction; CTA feels inviting rather than urgent.
ENVIRONMENT TENDENCY: terrace, white-wall space, pool/water, alley, open residence, travel-like space.
AVOID: forcing a literal sea view or tourism cliché.
COMPRESSED STYLE LINE: Mediterranean holiday advertising with luminous blue-white sun, warm skin, open framing, natural following, and visible air and wind.
