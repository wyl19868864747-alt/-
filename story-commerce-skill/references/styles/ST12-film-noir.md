# ST12 — Film-Noir Dark Cinema

VISUAL CORE: mysterious, dramatic, graphic tension built through shadow, silhouette, reflection, and framing.
EXPOSURE / COLOR: low-key but product/face readability preserved; black-and-white or controlled blue-black/amber.
LIGHT: hard side/back light, blinds/shadow patterns, wet reflections when motivated.
CAMERA / TEXTURE: slow push, static pressure composition, silhouette profile, selective close-up, hard reveal cut.
HOOK EXPRESSION BIAS: unresolved visual tension, suspicious object/action, controlled reveal.
STORY / CTA EXPRESSION BIAS: sparse dialogue, deliberate tension; CTA should resolve the mystery rather than become generic hard-sell.
ENVIRONMENT TENDENCY: interior, night, city, minimal space translated through light.
AVOID: automatic crime, weapons, horror, or making the whole image unreadably dark.
COMPRESSED STYLE LINE: Film-noir dark cinema with controlled low-key contrast, hard silhouette light and reflections, slow pressure-building movement, and a clean reveal.
