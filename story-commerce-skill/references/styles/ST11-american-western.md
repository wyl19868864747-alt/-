# ST11 — American Western Frontier

VISUAL CORE: rugged, grounded, tactile, weighty; land, work, weather, and material strength.
EXPOSURE / COLOR: strong sun, earth brown, denim blue, old wood, leather, metal.
LIGHT: hard realistic daylight with honest shadows.
CAMERA / TEXTURE: low angles, wide environment, telephoto compression, steady follow, material/action close-ups.
HOOK EXPRESSION BIAS: physical task, weight, durability impression, environment challenge, decisive action.
STORY / CTA EXPRESSION BIAS: concise and grounded; CTA feels practical and confident.
ENVIRONMENT TENDENCY: yard, garage, workshop, road, farm, rugged architecture.
AVOID: automatic cowboys, horses, guns, saloons, or crime.
COMPRESSED STYLE LINE: American Western frontier visual language with hard sun, earth and worn materials, low-angle weight, wide environments, and grounded physical action.
