# ST02 — Quiet-Luxury Premium Advertising

VISUAL CORE: calm, restrained, expensive through order, material, spacing, and attention.
EXPOSURE / COLOR: clean mid/high exposure, low-saturation neutrals; do not drift unnecessarily dark.
LIGHT: soft directional contour light and controlled material highlights.
CAMERA / TEXTURE: stable slow push, macro detail, short precise slides, generous negative space.
HOOK EXPRESSION BIAS: visual precision, product detail, controlled reveal rather than loud conflict.
STORY / CTA EXPRESSION BIAS: minimal dialogue, low-density persuasion, elegant CTA or brand close.
ENVIRONMENT TENDENCY: clean real spaces with refined material order.
AVOID: gold-for-luxury clichés, clutter, forced hype, heavy noir shadows.
COMPRESSED STYLE LINE: Quiet-luxury advertising with bright restrained neutrals, refined material light, precise slow camera movement, and generous negative space.
